# Postposition hâ in gaddi 

## Here is how "I am eating an apple" changes depending on position of 'hâ'

| gaddi | notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| au~ sEo hâ khandA* | what am i eating? |
| au~ hâ sEo khandA | who is eating the apple? | 
| au~ sEo khandA hâ | what am i doing with the apple? |
<br>
* '*' most common way to state a fact that i am eating an apple.
