# baeh(sit)
## example: iRi mat baeh | don't sit here  
| suffix |  word + suffix | meaning | example using word + suffix | meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
|**1.** â | baehâ | formal masculine or plural | iRi baehâ | Sit here (formal or you all) |
|**2.** dâ/di/dae | baehdâ | is sitting | uRi nIyae baehdâ/i <br> uRi nInae baehdae | He/She is not sitting there<br> They are not sitting there |
|**3.** indâ | baehIndâ | ability or will to sit | ett nâ baehIndâ | I can't sit here (inability or unwilling) |
|**4.** Àl/Àlâ | bahÀl | make them sit(informal/formal) | tesio bahÀl<br>tesio bahÀlâ | make him sit(informally said to a person your own age or younger)<br> make him sit(formally said to an elder person to make them do the task) |
|**5.** Urâ/Uri/Urae | baehUrâ | sitting | ciRi àllae puriâ baehUri | the sparrow is sitting on the nest |
|**6.** i | baehi | sit | baehi karae rotti khAi lE <br>baehi-baehi khiji go | eat your food after sitting down <br> I am tired from sitting so much | 
|**7.** lâ/li/lae | baehlâ | will sit | picchae baehla | (i will (masc.)) sit later(maybe) | 
|**8** ìllâ | baehìllâ | able to sit down | mu~ thau~ baehIllâ | will i be able to sit down |
|**9** INâ | baehINâ | able to sit down | mu~ thau baehINâae nIyae ta | i would not be able to sit down for sure |
|**10** | Nâ | baehNâ | will sit | mu-dhu na baehNâ iRi | I will not sit here(sure) |
|**11** | Ùra | baehÙra | sit | taes thau~ baehÙrâe nIyae ta | he wasn't able to sit down |
|**12** | u/i/ae | baehu | sat | ett na baehu | (did he) not sit here? |
|**13** | u~ | baehu~ | sit | au~ kaRi baehu~ | where should i sit |

# os:(climb down)
## example: inmae os | climb down from this side 
| suffix |  word + suffix | meaning | example using word + suffix | meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
|**1.** â | ossâ | formal masculine or plural | inmae ossâ | come down from this side(formal or masc plural) |
|**2.** dâ/di/dae | osdâ | is coming down | kinmaea osdâ | from which way are you coming down |
|**3.** indâ | osìndâ | ability or will to come down | mun~ thau nIyae osìndâ | I am incapable to come down |
|**4.** Àl/Àlâ | osÀl | make them come down(informal/formal) | tesio ossÀl<br>tesio ossÀlâ | make him come down(informally said to a person your own age or younger)<br> make him come down(formally said to an elder person to make them do the task) |
|**5.** Ùrâ | osUrâ | came down | unmae osUrae nIyae | (i) was unsuccesfful from coming down from there |
|**6.** i | òssi | climb down | òssi leyâ<br>pAERi òssi-òssi karae na~ bhuci gaI | try to come down (take your time) <br> I am tired from coming down so many stairs | 
|**7.** lâ/li/lae | oslâ | will come down | kinmae oslâ | from which side will they come down |
|**8** ìllâ | osìllâ | able to come down | mu~ thau osìllâ inmae(bhalâ) | will i be able to make it down from here(bhalâ is used when speaker is unsure of the outcome and is often pessimistic about it)|
|**9** INâ | osINâ | able to come down | mu~ thau osINâae nIyae ta | i would not be able to come down for sure |
|**10** | Nâ | osNâ | will come down | baNà~ bannâo bannhi karae osNâ | (i) will come down after tying ban oak in the forest |
|**11** | u/i/ae | ossu | climbed down | kinmae ossu | from what side did you come down |
|**12** | U~ | ossU~ | climb down | kinmae ossU~ | from what side should i come down |
