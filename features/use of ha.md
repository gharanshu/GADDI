# hâ : is/am/are 

## english: "I am eating an apple" 

### singular (informal)
| gaddi | spoken | note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| au~ sEo hâ khandA* | au~ sEoâ khandA | what am i eating? |
| au~ hâ sEo khandA | au~â sEo khandA | who is eating the apple? | 
| au~ sEo khandA hâ | au~ sEo khandAâ | what am i doing with the apple? |
| hâ kea khandA tu? | what are you eating(emphasised) |

### plural (formal)

| gaddi | spoken | note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| tusE sEo hin/hinnE khandE | tusaE sEon/nE khandE | what am i eating? |
| tusE hinnE/hin sEo khandA | tussaEn:/nE sEo khandE | who is eating the apple? | 
| tusE sEo khandA hinnae/hin | tusE sEo khandEn:/nE | what am i doing with the apple? |
| hin ke hinnae/hin khandE tusE? | hin kEn:/nE khandE tusE?  | what are you guys eating(emphasised) |

<br>
* '*' standard sentence - stating a fact 
* i blv hinnE is just more emphasised version of hin
