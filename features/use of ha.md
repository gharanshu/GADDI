# hâ in gaddi 

## english: "I am eating an apple" 

| gaddi | note |
| :--- | :--- |
| au~ sEo hâ khandA* | what am i eating? |
| au~ hâ sEo khandA | who is eating the apple? | 
| au~ sEo khandA hâ | what am i doing with the apple? |
| hâ kea khandA tu? | what are you eating(emphasised) |
<br>
* '*' standard setence.
