# SENTENCEs in gaddi-sal dialect(wetsern pahadi, northi indo aryan)

| pronoun | demostrative determiner | noun | auxillary | meanign |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| so | es | gharA~ | hâ | he/she is in this house |
| so | I~A | gharA~ | hâ | he/she is in these houses |
| so | es | gharA~ | hinn | they/she/he(plural or formal) are in this house |
| so | I~A | gharA~ | hinn | they/he/she(plural or formal) are in these houses |
| so | es | gharA~ | thu | he was in this house |
| so | I~A | gharA~ | thu | he was in these houses |
| so | es | gharA~ | thi | she was in this house |
| so | I~A | gharA~ | thi | she was in these houses |
| so | es | gharA~ | thiæ | they/he/she(plural or formal) were in this house |
| so | I~A | gharA~ | thiæ | they/she/he(Plural or formal) were in these houses |
| so | es | gharA~ | bhUNâ | he will be in this house | 
| so | I~A | gharA~ | bhUNâ | he will be in these houses |
| so | es | gharA~ | bhUNi | she will be in this house |
| so | I~A | gharA~ | bhUNi | she will be in these houses |
| so | es | gharA~ | bhUNε | they/he/she(plural or formal) will be in this house |
| so | I~A | gharA~ | bhUNε | they/she/he(Plural or formal) will be in these houses |

* pragmatically all the future one are more like you'll prolly find them there rather that they will be there as in future.
