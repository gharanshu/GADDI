# BASIC SENTENCES IN GADDI
| SENTENCE | TRANSLATION |
| The sheep is here | bhaeR idi ha |
| I see the sheep | mei~ bhaeR heri |
| the sheep ate the grass | bhaeRae ghA khai leu |
| give water to the sheep | bhaeRao pANi piyA |
| he took the wool from the sheep | tini bhaerA thau u~nn lei |
| he is sleeping inside the house | so gharA~ sutturA |
