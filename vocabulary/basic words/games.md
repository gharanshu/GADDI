## childhood games and folklore

## GAMES
| Game | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| luk:-lukANi | hide and seek from root luk:(to hide)  |
| kukU hae teri teri teri | peek - a - boo |
| HI-HI ambarâ dhapâ thallæ chOg | snow-show the sky, there is bait(grain) under a trap (proverb to catch bird during snow) |
| uTUru-maTUru rAm kurhARi lekar nha~h | kids gather in a circle, sit down with both hands in front in a punch shape kept on ground a person recite this sentence and when the last word falls on someones hand they hide it behind themselves then it is followed until all hands are eliminated. the person who have their hands elimiated runs to other side the last two to be eliminated act as travellers that are sick one carrying other and others who ran away act as housekeepers who would be hosting the two. The two beg for food and water in different plates like brass(kUTi), sunnâ(gold) and chAndi(silver) the travellers poses themselves as upper caste to eat for few turns in nice utensils then they say they are lower caste randomly and are chased away or beaten by the hosts they can come over and pretend to be new guests with new names. this would over and over again until they decide to replay the entire game |
| hAkkhri-miTTu | one person is blindfolded and have to catch others based on sounds first to get caught is the next it |
| guTkalu-Amlâ/kikkli | hold hand and spin in circles one saying guTkalu other replying Amlâ and jumping on spot too while doing it |
| huDDu | game with numbers on groud drawn using chalk (khàDDi) players have to jump on those blocks as told by others or in a sequence |
| piTThu-garam | two teams play one has to make a stone mound other use a cloth(guddaR) ball to hit on the bakcs of  the players of the team trying to build the mound. The ones who got hit with a ball are eliminated and can't built a tower if they are successful in building a mound that team wins but if everyone is eliminated the defenders win | 
| gATæ | game with small pebbles have to catch the pebbles on front and back of hand while throwin them up and increasing count each time those who fail either get pinched or slapped the stones used are the semi-traslucent pebbles mostly called hukrá |
| kAji-kAji, ji hazUr, in dOnO~ me se pakRo chOr | a game where kAji rAjâ rAni and chOr etc. are written on paper slips and folded. the slips are then tossed with players getting one slip each which they see and dont reveal to others. then the one who got rAjâ asks "kAji-kAji", to which kAji has to reply "jI hajUr" then raja ask to find the theif if they guessed wrong kAji get 0 point and chOr get the points equivalent to kAji's 800. if guessed right kAji get his points and chOr gets a zero rAjâ is 1000, rAni 500 etc. over few number of turns the final score is added for each player to see who has highest score, they are the winner |
